Installed

If your printer was connected, it is now set up as HP LaserJet 1020. Print from any app with Cmd-P.

Not connected yet? Plug it in, open System Settings → Printers & Scanners, click Add Printer, and pick HP LaserJet 1020. The driver is selected automatically.

To remove the driver, double-click /Library/Printers/hp1020/uninstall.command.

Problems? Details are in /Library/Printers/hp1020/install.log and at github.com/UtkarshChandel/hp1020-macos-driver.
